Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about our IT, data center, and security staffing services.

What does CoreGenix's IT staffing service include?
CoreGenix provides skilled staffing across three areas: IT infrastructure (network and systems engineers), data center operations (DC support and remote hands), and cyber security (SOC analysts and compliance staff) — deployed on-site, remote, or project-based.
Does CoreGenix provide data center staffing?
Yes, CoreGenix provides data center support staffing including DC operations engineers, remote/smart hands support, and infrastructure monitoring staff for server rooms and data center environments.
Can CoreGenix staff cyber security roles like SOC analysts?
Yes, CoreGenix provides cyber security staffing including SOC analysts, security engineers, and GRC/compliance professionals to support security operations and audit requirements.
Can you staff for short-term IT projects?
Yes. CoreGenix scales IT, data center, and security engineering teams up or down for migrations, rollouts, audits, and other time-bound technical initiatives.
Do you provide IT staffing services across India?
Yes, CoreGenix delivers IT, data center, and cyber security staffing in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Jaipur, and across India with PAN-India operations.
What is the difference between on-site and remote IT staffing?
On-site staffing places dedicated engineers at your premises during working hours, while remote/hybrid staffing combines remote support with periodic on-site visits — CoreGenix offers both models depending on your operational needs.
Are your engineers certified?
Yes, depending on the role, engineers are screened for relevant certifications such as CCNA/CCNP for networking, CompTIA Server+/Linux+ for systems, and CEH, CISSP, or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor for security and compliance roles.
How quickly can CoreGenix deploy IT or security staff?
Typical deployment timelines range from a few days for pre-vetted roles to 2–3 weeks for specialized security or niche technical positions, depending on the skill set and engagement model required.
