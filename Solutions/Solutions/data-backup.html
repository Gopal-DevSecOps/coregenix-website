What are data backup solutions?

Data backup solutions cover the automated copying of servers, databases, applications, and endpoint data to a safe location so it can be restored after loss. CoreGenix delivers this using a 3-2-1 strategy — on-premise backup for fast local restore, cloud backup for offsite protection, and regular restore testing with monitoring and alerting, for businesses across India.

Two concepts worth understanding

Retention scheduling and ransomware resistance come up in every backup conversation — here's the short version of each.

GFS Backup Retention

GFS (Grandfather-Father-Son) is a rotation scheme that keeps daily backups (Son) for a short window, weekly backups (Father) for longer, and monthly backups (Grandfather) for extended retention — balancing storage cost against how far back you can recover.

Ransomware-Resistant Backup

Standard network-connected backups can be encrypted by ransomware along with production data. Ransomware-resistant backup uses immutable storage (cannot be altered or deleted for a set period) or air-gapped copies (physically or logically disconnected) so at least one clean copy always survives an attack.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about backup strategy, cost, and ransomware protection.

What is the best backup strategy?
A 3-2-1 strategy is the industry standard: at least three copies of your data, on two different types of storage media, with one copy stored offsite. CoreGenix implements this as the default backup architecture for clients.
How often should data be backed up?
Backup frequency should match how much data your business can afford to lose, known as its Recovery Point Objective (RPO). Critical systems may need continuous or hourly backups, while less critical data may only need daily or weekly backups.
How much does a backup solution cost in India?
Backup solution costs depend on data volume, retention period, and whether backups are on-premise, cloud, or both. CoreGenix offers a free backup assessment to scope requirements before providing a fixed quote.
What is the difference between on-premise and cloud backup?
On-premise backup stores copies locally, offering fast restore speeds but no protection if the site itself is affected. Cloud backup stores copies offsite, protecting against site-wide disasters and ransomware, though restore speed depends on internet bandwidth. Most businesses use both together.
What is GFS backup retention?
GFS (Grandfather-Father-Son) is a backup rotation scheme that retains daily backups (Son) for a short period, weekly backups (Father) for a medium period, and monthly backups (Grandfather) for long-term retention, balancing storage cost against recovery flexibility.
How does backup protect against ransomware?
Ransomware-resistant backup relies on immutable or air-gapped copies that cannot be encrypted or deleted by an attacker, combined with offsite storage and regular restore testing to confirm recovery works even if production systems and connected backups are compromised.
How do I know if my backups will actually restore?
The only reliable way to know a backup will restore is to test it regularly through scheduled restore drills, not just confirm that the backup job completed successfully. CoreGenix performs regular restore testing and provides written proof of recoverability.
