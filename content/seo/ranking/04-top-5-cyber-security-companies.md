## 3. How to Evaluate Cyber Security Companies (The Framework)

Before comparing companies, score every vendor on these five factors:

1. **Experience & specialization** — How many years? Do they have experts (not just resellers)?
2. **Breadth of services** — Can they cover assessment, monitoring, response and compliance? Or will you need multiple vendors?
3. **Proven results** — Delivered projects, retained clients, real testimonials.
4. **Response capability** — Do they have 24x7 monitoring and incident response?
5. **Industry fit** — Do they understand your sector — including OT if you're industrial?
