## 5. FAQ
**What is an AMC?**
An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is an agreement where a provider maintains your IT equipment for a year — covering preventive and corrective maintenance at a fixed cost.

**What equipment does an IT AMC cover?**
Servers, storage, networking hardware, firewalls, desktops, laptops, printers and sometimes software licenses, depending on the agreement.
