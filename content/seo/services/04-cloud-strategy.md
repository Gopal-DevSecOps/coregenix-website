## 5. FAQ
**What is a cloud strategy?**
A cloud strategy is a plan that defines which workloads go to cloud, where (public, private, hybrid), how, and at what cost and risk.

**How long does cloud migration take?**
It depends on complexity. Simple workloads can move in weeks; full estates take longer. Your roadmap defines realistic phases.
