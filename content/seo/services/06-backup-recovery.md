## 5. FAQ
**What is data backup and recovery?**
Backup is copying your data to a safe location; recovery is restoring it after loss. Together they protect you from downtime and data loss.

**How often should I back up?**
As often as your business can afford to lose changes. CoreGenix helps you define the right backup frequency and retention.

**What is the difference between backup and DR?**
Backup restores data; disaster recovery restores whole systems and operations at scale. Most businesses need both.
