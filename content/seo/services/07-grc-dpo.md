## 5. FAQ
**What is GRC?**
GRC stands for Governance, Risk and Compliance — the processes that keep an organization accountable, manage its risks, and meet regulatory requirements.

**What does a Data Protection Officer (DPO) do?**
A DPO oversees data protection strategy, ensures compliance with privacy laws, and serves as the point of accountability for personal data handling.

**Why do I need GRC consulting?**
GRC gives you a structured, documented approach to risk and compliance — reducing fines, audit failures and reputational risk.
