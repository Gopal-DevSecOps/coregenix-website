## 5. FAQ
**What is a cyber security audit?**
A cyber security audit is an independent review of your security policies, controls and systems to identify weaknesses and compliance gaps.

**How often should I audit my security?**
Annually is standard, plus after major changes. Clients with compliance obligations may need more frequent audits.

**What's the difference between an audit and VAPT?**
An audit reviews controls, policies and processes. VAPT actively tests systems for exploitable vulnerabilities. They complement each other.
